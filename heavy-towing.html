Specialized Heavy Vehicle Towing in Ghaziabad

When a commercial vehicle experiences mechanical breakdown or a collision, ordinary tow trucks cannot handle the job. Heavy towing requires high-capacity equipment, engineered underlift systems, high-tensile winching gear, and experienced recovery operators. Classic Tow Service provides dependable 24/7 heavy vehicle towing throughout Ghaziabad and surrounding highway corridors.

Our heavy recovery services cater to light and heavy commercial vehicles (LCV and HCV), cargo trucks, intercity buses, tourist coaches, trailers, tippers, and construction equipment carriers. Whether your fleet vehicle has suffered differential failure, a seized axle, air brake lockup, or engine breakdown, our operators execute safe axle lifts and secure tie-downs to protect your vehicle and payload during transit.

Complex incidents such as rollovers, jackknifed tractor-trailers, or vehicles trapped in steep roadside ditches demand specialized heavy lifting capability. For such demanding operations, we collaborate closely with RSA Crane Service, our crane and heavy-lift partner. This partnership enables us to mobilize heavy-duty mobile cranes alongside our recovery units for uprighting overturned chassis, ditch extrications, and site clearances with surgical precision.

The heavy recovery process begins the moment you call: we assess your vehicle type, gross weight, cargo situation, and road environment. Our dispatch team sends the right recovery wrecker, secures clearance, and tows your vehicle to your company terminal, authorized dealer workshop, or commercial repair hub. With Classic Tow Service, you get prompt response, round-the-clock commercial support, and complete operational transparency.
