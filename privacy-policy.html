Privacy Policy

How Classic Tow Service collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data when you visit our website or use our towing and roadside assistance services.

Effective Date: 12 November 2026 | Last Updated: 12 November 2026

Notice: This policy should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before publishing to ensure full compliance with applicable national and local data protection regulations.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Classic Tow Service ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the website https://classictowservice.com and provide 24/7 vehicle towing, breakdown assistance, flatbed transport, and on-demand roadside recovery across Ghaziabad and surrounding regions.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and store it, under what circumstances we may share it, and the rights you have concerning your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp, contact forms, or email, or when you use our roadside services, we may collect the following personal details:

  • Contact details: Full name, telephone/mobile number, and email address.
  • Location information: Real-time GPS location pin, street address, landmarks, pickup location, and vehicle destination (drop-off address).
  • Vehicle details: Vehicle make, model, variant, registration number, transmission type (manual or automatic), vehicle condition, and key status.
  • Enquiry and service information: Nature of the breakdown or incident, towing notes, and communication logs exchanged with our dispatchers.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and pages visited on classictowservice.com collected via automated cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data exclusively for lawful operational purposes, including:

  • Service dispatch and fulfillment: To quickly dispatch the nearest appropriate tow truck or roadside technician to your location and transport your vehicle safely to the desired destination.
  • Customer communication: To confirm quotes, send driver arrival estimates, update you on transit status, and follow up regarding service completion.
  • Safety and incident management: To ensure accurate vehicle handling, confirm authorization for towing, and assist with necessary accident reports if requested.
  • Service improvement: To analyse operational efficiency, monitor dispatcher response times, and enhance our website performance and user experience.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes standard browser cookies and local storage tokens to deliver fundamental site features, remember cookie banner consent status, and understand how visitors interact with our content.

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. Essential cookies enable core security and navigation functionality. You can configure your browser settings to reject cookies or notify you when cookies are sent; however, certain site features may have reduced functionality if cookies are disabled.

5. Google Analytics & Google Ads

We may implement Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Ads conversion tracking to measure anonymous traffic patterns, monitor marketing campaign effectiveness, and understand user queries. These tools collect anonymized data such as device identifiers, browser types, approximate geographic location, session duration, and referral sources.

You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing your visits across websites by downloading and installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can also customize Google advertising settings through Google's Ads Settings portal.

6. Third-Party Links & External Services

Our website contains links to external partners, including RSA Crane Service (https://rsacraneservice.com/) and Google Business (Google Maps Listing). Please be aware that once you click on a link to a third-party website, their own privacy practices and terms govern your interaction. Classic Tow Service has no control over and assumes no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any external sites.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information strictly under the following limited circumstances:

  • Service contractors and partners: With our assigned tow truck drivers, roadside technicians, or crane operators (such as RSA Crane Service) strictly to the extent required to execute your towing or recovery request.
  • Legal obligations: When required by applicable law, court order, traffic police, or governmental regulation during accident investigations or traffic clearance mandates.
  • Protection of rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Classic Tow Service, our operators, customers, or the general public.

8. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and job-related personal data only for 12 to 24 months or as reasonably necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy, resolve billing disputes, maintain audit trails, and comply with statutory legal requirements under Indian law.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable local laws, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request confirmation of whether we hold personal data concerning you.
  • The right to access and receive a copy of your personal data.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data from our operational records, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at support@classictowservice.com with the subject line "Data Privacy Request."

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and towing services are intended for vehicle owners, commercial fleet managers, and licensed drivers. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted personal details through our website, please notify us immediately and we will promptly remove such records.

11. Security Safeguards

We maintain appropriate physical, electronic, and administrative precautions to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, please remember that no transmission over the internet or cellular network is guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational changes, service expansion, or regulatory adjustments. Whenever changes occur, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact Us
