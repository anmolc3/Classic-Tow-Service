Frequently Asked Questions: Heavy Towing in Hapur

What commercial vehicles are covered under heavy towing in Hapur?

Our heavy towing fleet handles high-tonnage commercial goods vehicles (LCVs, MCVs, HCVs), multi-axle freight trailers, transit buses, interstate coaches, concrete mixers, and construction equipment haulers.

Do you disconnect the drive shaft or release air brakes before towing in Hapur?

Yes. Our experienced mechanics disconnect the drive axle shaft on commercial trucks before towing to prevent costly differential gear and transmission damage, and connect auxiliary air lines to safely release spring brake lockups.

What if an overturned truck or trailer requires a heavy crane lift in Hapur?

Through our direct partnership with RSA Crane Service (https://rsacraneservice.com/), we mobilize high-capacity mobile hydraulic cranes on-site for tandem uprighting, load balancing, and heavy winch extractions.

Are your heavy wreckers permitted to operate on expressways around Hapur?

Yes, our heavy wreckers and commercial recovery operators comply with all regional transport authority safety regulations and express transit clearances across Hapur corridors.

How is heavy towing pricing calculated in Hapur?

Heavy towing costs are based on gross vehicle weight (GVW), whether the vehicle is loaded or unloaded, the distance to destination workshop, and whether crane lift assistance is required. Upfront quotes are provided before dispatch.
