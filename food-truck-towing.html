Protecting Your Mobile Business & Kitchen Equipment

A food truck is much more than an ordinary motor vehicle; it is your entire commercial kitchen, stockroom, and revenue stream on wheels. Food trucks are inherently heavy, have high centres of gravity, long wheelbases, and house delicate commercial appliances like espresso machines, deep fryers, gas burner ranges, refrigerators, and heavy auxiliary generators. Standard passenger tow trucks simply do not possess the tonnage capacity or bed length to transport a food truck safely.

Classic Tow Service operates specialised heavy flatbed trucks and commercial recovery haulers built specifically for high-capacity applications across Ghaziabad. Our operators are trained to recognize the unique weight distributions and vulnerable clearance points of mobile kitchen builds. Whether built on a Force Traveller chassis, Tata 407, Ashok Leyland Dost, or a custom-fabricated trailer chassis, we match the exact rig needed for zero-stress transport.

We take meticulous care during the loading and strapping process. Using gradual hydraulic winch lines and extended commercial-grade loading ramps, we eliminate violent shocks that could displace kitchen countertops, stress gas connections, or damage custom exterior branding wraps. Four-point chassis anchoring and high-tension wheel chocks keep the entire food truck immovably secure during transit.

Because every hour your food truck is out of service represents lost business, we offer 24/7 priority emergency dispatch. We transport stranded food trucks directly to commercial diesel repair facilities, custom fabricators, food truck parks, or back to your catering commissary.
