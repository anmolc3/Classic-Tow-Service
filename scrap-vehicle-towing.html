Professional Scrap & End-of-Life Vehicle Removal in Ghaziabad

Unwanted, end-of-life, and stationary scrap vehicles consume valuable parking space and can become eyesores or safety hazards in residential housing societies and commercial complexes. Classic Tow Service offers organized, reliable scrap vehicle towing across Ghaziabad, removing non-functional cars, accident write-offs, abandoned clunkers (with valid owner consent), and decommissioned commercial autos.

Scrap vehicles frequently present unique towing obstacles: seized braking systems, rusted undercarriages, missing rims, deflated tyres, or dead electrical systems that leave steering columns immobilized. Our flatbed recovery fleet is equipped with high-powered winches, heavy snatch blocks, and specialized wheel skids designed to gently extract immobile vehicles from tight basement car parks, narrow gated alleys, and overgrown lots without damaging surrounding structures.

Please note that customer compliance and proper documentation are essential. Classic Tow Service is an independent towing and logistical transport provider. The vehicle owner or authorized custodian is responsible for maintaining valid ownership records, including the Registration Certificate (RC) copy and government photo identification, prior to transport. We do not purchase vehicles or make unverified cash offers; instead, we provide dependable, damage-free flatbed transportation straight to your chosen authorized vehicle recycling facility (RVSF), scrapyard, or dismantling garage.

Scheduling a scrap vehicle pickup with us is seamless: provide your vehicle details, its current physical condition, and its pickup address. We confirm our transparent transport charges upfront and dispatch a flatbed wrecker at a time that suits your schedule. Clear out unused garage space effortlessly with Classic Tow Service.
