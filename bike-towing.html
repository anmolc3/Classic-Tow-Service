Specialised Motorcycle & Scooter Towing Across Ghaziabad

Two-wheelers demand dedicated transport methods. Laying a motorcycle or scooter on its side in an open pickup truck causes severe damage to fairings, bends the handlebars, scratches paint, and frequently leaks battery acid or engine fuel. At Classic Tow Service, we treat your two-wheeler with professional care using specialised bike carriers and upright transport systems.

Our tow vehicles are equipped with purpose-built wheel chocks and soft-webbing ratchet straps. When loading, the front wheel rolls into a secure locking cradle that holds the motorcycle perfectly upright. Multi-point tension straps are then fastened to structural frame points rather than delicate plastic bodywork or wiring harnesses. This guarantees zero tipping, zero metal-to-metal rubbing, and complete stability throughout transit across Ghaziabad.

We handle all categories of two-wheelers. Whether you ride a daily commuter (Hero Splendor, Honda Shine, Bajaj Pulsar, TVS Apache), a premium cruiser (Royal Enfield Classic 350, Hunter, Himalayan), a modern electric scooter (Ather, Ola, Chetak), an automatic scooter (Activa, Access), or a high-performance superbike (Kawasaki, KTM, BMW Motorrad, Triumph), our team possesses the technical know-how to load and secure your machine properly.

Our roadside assistance fleet operates 24/7 to solve two-wheeler breakdowns wherever you are: flat tyres without tubes, broken drive chains, electrical and starter failures, clutch cable snaps, drained batteries, or post-collision damage. We transport your vehicle directly to an authorised dealership, your local mechanic, or right to your residence.
