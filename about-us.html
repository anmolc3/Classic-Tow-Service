Who We Are

Built on Trust, Speed & Precision Recovery

Founded with a commitment to eliminate the stress and uncertainty associated with roadside emergencies, Classic Tow Service has grown into Ghaziabad's most reputable 24-hour towing and roadside recovery network.

Whether assisting everyday commuters stranded by a dead battery, transporting luxury low-clearance sports cars, or managing complex commercial accident recoveries, our operators treat every vehicle with uncompromising care.

In partnership with RSA Crane Service, our unified fleet includes ground-level hydraulic zero-degree flatbeds, heavy-duty commercial wheel-lifts, and industrial mobile cranes.

Our Core Principles of Operation

Why drivers, corporate fleets, and emergency services in Ghaziabad trust Classic Tow Service.

Zero Damage Policy

We use heavy-duty nylon tyre straps and true ground-level hydraulic slide beds to guarantee zero scratches on bumpers, splitters, rims, or chassis.

15–30 Min Response

Our centralized dispatch hub routes the closest available recovery vehicle across Ghaziabad, minimizing wait times in unsafe roadside conditions.

Upfront Pricing

No sudden fuel surcharges or surprise fees. You receive a guaranteed quote based strictly on vehicle category and total towing distance before we depart.

Certified Crew

Every driver and roadside technician is trained in winching safety, motorway extraction, EV battery dynamics, and customer care.
